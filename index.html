My Journey

The Story Behind Me.

A journey of change, challenges, and personal growth. Here's a little story about who I was and who I'm working to become.

🧒
CHAPTER 01

My Childhood

During my elementary days, I always went outside to play with the kids in our neighborhood. I loved having fun, spending time with friends, and making memories. Those were some of the happiest moments of my childhood.

🌍
CHAPTER 02

The Pandemic

Then COVID-19 came, and we were all forced to stay at home and limit our interactions with others. This period lasted for about two years, and life changed in many ways. I didn't realize how much this experience would affect me.

💭
CHAPTER 03

A Different Me

After the pandemic, I noticed that I had changed. I used to be an extroverted person, but I became more introverted and struggled with social anxiety. Sometimes, I felt frustrated because interacting with others no longer felt as natural as before.

🌱
CHAPTER 04

Moving Forward

As time passed, I began trying to overcome my difficulties with socializing. I'm still learning how to become more comfortable around other people. I know growth takes time, and I want to keep improving and become a better version of myself.

"My past may have changed me, but it doesn't have to define who I become."

— Kurt Bocao
